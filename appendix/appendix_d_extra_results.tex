\section{Archived Confirmatory Evaluation Files}
\label{app:extra_results}

The main text reports the predeclared H1--H4 estimands and the tables needed
for substantive interpretation. The complete machine-readable confirmatory
outputs are retained in the repository under
\path{outputs/confirmatory/}. They are archival results rather than tables
recomputed when the manuscript is compiled.

\begin{description}[leftmargin=3.2cm,style=nextline]
    \item[\textbf{H1}]
    \path{h1_information_gain_detail.csv} contains the eligible paired
    forecast-origin observations and
    \path{h1_information_gain_summary.csv} contains the 28 adjacent-stage
    summary comparisons.

    \item[\textbf{H2}]
    \path{h2_stage_policy_detail.csv} contains the paired stage-policy and
    fixed-comparator forecast-origin observations and
    \path{h2_stage_policy_summary.csv} contains all 36 target--stage cells,
    including the 25 exact same-model ties.

    \item[\textbf{H3}]
    \path{h3_vintage_detail.csv} contains the paired real-time and
    latest-vintage forecast-origin observations;
    \path{h3_vintage_summary.csv} contains the 36 stage-level summaries;
    \path{h3_vintage_target_summary.csv} contains the eight target-level
    complete-stage summaries; and
    \path{h3_vintage_sample_audit.csv} records the H3 sample construction.

    \item[\textbf{H4}]
    \path{h4_interval_detail.csv} contains the forecast-level interval
    results; \path{h4_interval_summary.csv} contains the stage--method
    summaries; \path{h4_interval_method_comparisons.csv} contains the paired
    interval-score comparisons; and
    \path{h4_interval_eligibility_audit.csv} records the prior-only
    calibration-history eligibility checks.
\end{description}

The confirmatory archives have corresponding manifests in the same directory.
The manifests record the frozen design or policy anchors, source backtest
identifiers, row counts, analysis-script hashes, and SHA-256 hashes of the
archived output files. The H1--H4 results reported in
Section~\ref{sec:results} are summaries of these immutable archives; this
appendix does not introduce additional bootstrap, conditional-predictive-
ability, model-ranking, or post-hoc specification results.
